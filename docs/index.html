One entry per person per division (their best); try N shows how many times they submitted in that division.

self-reported: the submission carries the tasks and the candidate's answers. The bot re-checks them in code, without calling a model: quoted evidence against the answers, output contracts, this quarter's attack line-up and wording, and the leak detectors. A verdict that quotes text the answers do not contain, or that calls a leaked answer defended, fails. The interview itself is not re-run and no model re-grades it, so answers written by hand to look plausible, or a verdict that misjudges a real answer, would still pass.

unverified: a submission without answers (made by an older version of the skill, or too long to fit in an issue). Only the document screening could be re-checked, so it is listed below every re-checked entry. N/M passes is the submitter's own count of repeated interviews and is not checked. Entries re-run by the maintainer (verified) do not exist yet. Every spec is public under docs/submissions, so anyone can re-run it.
